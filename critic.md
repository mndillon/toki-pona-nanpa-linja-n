# critic on the nanpa linja proposal

So the system looks sophisticated but I think what people generally dislike is a avanlanch of too much on them. 
So I'd say people want something they understand immediately and generally it helps if it isn't much. 

What Toki Pona really lacks is a way of saying times or to the power too. A way to express subtraction. A way
to express negative numbers, a way to express numbers lower than one. 

# 423 or compatibility with what people actually use now 

As for the phone number sample. I think this is how it would look in real life.

A: o toki tawa mi e nanpa pi ilo mu sina !
B: pona. sitelen nanpa wan li tu tu. sitelen nanpa tu li tu. sitelen nanpa tu wan li tu wan. ....

So basically it's quite easy to tell the value of one digit. 
One could even just separate the digits with the word sitelen and I think it would be understood out of context. 

# some thoughts of mine 

I think words for multiplication, subtraction, to the power of, or for negative numbers (below zero) should be found. 

A: lete seme la ni li lon ma. (how could is it outside?)
B: ni li jo lili luka lon anpa ala. (it has five degrees below zero )

A: tenpo seme la mama mama sina li lon ? (how old's your grandma ?)
B: ona li lon tenpo sike ale weka mute. (she's around for a hundred years minus twenty)

A: o toki tawa mi e ni: jan mute seme li lon ma ni? (tell me how many people are on earth?)
B: luka luka sike ale sewi kuka lon poka. (nearly 10 * 100^5 or around ten billions) 

A: mani seme la sina weka e ijo tan esun la sina wile pana tawa lawa ma lon ma Esalasi? (how high is the VAT in Austria?)
B: ni li kipisi wan pi kipisi luka. (it's one part of five or 20 %)
