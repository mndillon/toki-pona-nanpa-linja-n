Compact code derived from the nanpa-linja-n proper name. Most n and e letters are omitted; a no-value spacer keeps lowercase ee, and scientific notation keeps lowercase eko. ni li nimi lili tan nimi pi nanpa-linja-n. nanpa sona la nimi eko li awen.
