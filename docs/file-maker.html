Select and Lasso — move and edit artwork
1 Drag a box (Select) or freehand outline (Lasso) 2 Drag the selection to move it 3 Drag handles to resize it 4 Use the selection action buttons to edit it

Select editable ink or complete vector contours. Drag inside the selection to move it; drag the eight handles to resize. Corner handles preserve proportions (hold Shift to resize freely); side handles change one dimension. Select an area and press Fill to add ink or Eraser to remove ink immediately. The selection stays active for further editing.

More: Copy, cut, paste, duplicate, delete, rotate, flip and scale selected artwork. Arrow keys nudge by 1 unit (Shift: 10). Width changes affect only selected ink. Each completed drag is one Undo step. The faint grey source font is an uneditable guide; use Copy glyph to copy the original outlines. To change individual Bézier points, switch to Edit path.

Press ✕ Hide help to clear this panel. Reopen it anytime with ⓘ Help beside Drawing canvas.
Drawing with Bézier curvesReady to draw a new outline
1 Click to place points 2 Click + drag for curves 3 Click first/last point to close (inner outlines cut holes) 4 Close hole to make transparent

Click in the drawing area to place your first point.

Fill: open paths are guide lines; closed contours immediately fill black. To cut out the centre of a shape (like the letter o), draw an inner outline and click its first or last point to close it; fully enclosed outlines automatically become holes. You can also press Close hole to force a hole, or convert a finished outline with Make selected outline a hole. You can also choose a completed outline from the Outline menu and change its type to Hole, without clicking a tiny point. Choose Preview filled glyph to hide handles and see only the black ink and transparent holes.Sharp corner: click. Smooth curve: click and drag. Click the first or last point to close, or click and drag while closing to bend the final segment; Shift+click/drag forces a hole. The drag changes only the last segment, not the first one. Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo one point; Enter closes automatically, Shift+Enter closes as hole, Esc cancels; Alt + drag a handle adjusts it independently.
Drawing canvas
100%

Mouse-wheel or pinch to zoom · Space + drag to pan · F to fit · Shift constrains circles and squares · Select artwork to copy, paste, move, resize with handles, rotate, flip or scale · Fill: click inside a closed shape or select an area and press Fill · Eraser: select an area first to erase it immediately without losing the selection, or drag to erase freely · Curve: drag endpoints and adjust the purple bend handle; Line/Curve can start at a highlighted endpoint to extend the same path. Alt-drag an endpoint to move it, or use Edit path; Shift-select two endpoints and join them from the Paths palette · Undo/redo stays on your glyph or asks before returning to another glyph. Smooth: set the strength, then click Smooth outline to reduce bumps in existing curves. To smooth only part of a drawing, select that area first: the edges of your selection remain unchanged, and the result blends gradually into the surrounding ink. Undo reverses it. The slider also smooths Pencil strokes while drawing. The faint original glyph remains an uneditable reference.
