Proposal: fractional glyph scaling inside sitelen pona cartouches

Experimental community proposal

Overview. This proposal adds an optional typographic control for ordinary sitelen pona cartouches: a glyph may be followed by ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, or ¾ to draw that one glyph at the corresponding fractional scale inside the cartouche.

The fraction is a formatting instruction, not part of the proper name. It is not drawn. Full-size glyphs remain the default, and an author does not need to use scaling at all. The purpose is to give authors optional visual freedom to emphasize some glyphs and de-emphasize glyphs used mainly to help indicate pronunciation.

The same five controls may also be written with the ASCII aliases 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 2/3, and 3/4.

[kulupu⅔ :⅔ soweli,, pipi,, kala,, waso,, nena⅓ akesi]

Detailed proposal

1. Scope

Fractional scaling applies only inside ordinary sitelen pona cartouches. Outside a cartouche, the Unicode vulgar-fraction characters keep their ordinary visible meaning and are not formatting controls.

2. Available scale modifiers

Unicode controlASCII aliasEffect on the immediately preceding cartouche entry
¼1/4quarter scale
⅓1/3one-third scale
½1/2half scale
⅔2/3two-thirds scale
¾3/4three-quarter scale

3. Postfix rule

A modifier applies to the single immediately preceding cartouche entry. The modifier itself is not drawn.

4. Optional authorial emphasis

Scaling is optional and does not change the linguistic value of the glyph or the proper name. A conventional unscaled cartouche remains valid. The proposal only gives the author an additional visual distinction when desired.

5. ASCII aliases

The ASCII forms are input conveniences. Within an ordinary cartouche, jan1/2 and jan½ request the same half-scale rendering. Fonts using underscore-style Latin ligatures may likewise accept the corresponding no-space underscore form where required by that font's convention.

6. Compounds, punctuation and spacing

The modifier belongs to the preceding rendered cartouche entry. Existing compound syntax remains separate. Colon and middle-dot entries remain inside the cartouche without breaking it. Using vulgar-fraction scaling with IDEOGRAPHIC SPACE or zz may or may not work for any given font.

7. Tallies

Where comma is used for the existing cartouche tally convention, tallies may follow a fraction-modified glyph. The preceding glyph is scaled; the tally marks themselves are not scaled.

8. Literal cartouches

Fraction aliases are not interpreted as scaling controls inside literal-cartouche forms. Literal content remains literal.

9. Status

This page describes an experimental proposal and an implementation for people to test. It is not a claim that fractional cartouche scaling is part of the Common Sitelen Pona standard or an established community convention.
